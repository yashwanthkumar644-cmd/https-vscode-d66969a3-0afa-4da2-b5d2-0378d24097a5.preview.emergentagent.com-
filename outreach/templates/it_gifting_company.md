# Template: IT Gifting Company (corporate gifting pitch)

Only use once a company is in `tracker.csv` with `likely`/`confirmed` evidence — the email should
reference the *specific* occasion/evidence, not a guess.

Subject: `Ideas for {{company_name}}'s {{gift_occasion}} gifting`

```
Hi {{contact_name}},

Saw that {{company_name}} puts thought into {{gift_occasion}} gifting for the team
({{evidence_reference}}) — that stood out, most companies don't bother.

I help companies put together {{gift_occasion}} gifting (onboarding kits / festival hampers /
milestone gifts) that feel less generic than the usual branded-pen-and-mug set — curated,
on-brand, delivered pan-India.

Worth a quick 15-min call to see if it's a fit for {{company_name}}'s next round?

Best,
{{your_name}}
```

Personalization checklist:
- [ ] `{{evidence_reference}}` — a real, specific detail from `research_notes.md`, not invented.
      If there's no solid evidence yet, do not send this template — go verify first.
- [ ] Confirm `{{contact_name}}` is the right function (HR / People Ops / Employee Experience / Admin),
      not a generic info@ address.
- [ ] Never claim a company "gives gifts" as fact in the email if confidence is `unverified` —
      soften to a question instead, e.g. "curious how {{company_name}} approaches onboarding gifting".
